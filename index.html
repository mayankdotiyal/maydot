Engineering the atomic lattice of vanadium dioxide (VO₂) thin films — modulating chemical strain and dopant chemistry to tune the semiconductor–to–metal transition for energy-efficient smart windows.

Working in the laboratory of Prof. Hiromichi Ohta at RIES, Hokkaido University. My research bridges RF magnetron sputtering with first-principles Density Functional Theory (DFT) utilizing VASP and Quantum ESPRESSO to correlate synthesis conditions with structure, defects, and functional properties in inorganic oxide systems.
