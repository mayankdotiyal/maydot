About

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Research Institute for Electronic Science (RIES), Hokkaido University, working with Prof. Hiromichi Ohta. I completed my Ph.D. in Materials Engineering at IIT Gandhinagar under Prof. Emila Panda, where my doctoral work focused on chemical strain-induced structural and semiconductor-to-metal transitions in VO₂ thin films — combining RF magnetron sputtering with first-principles DFT (VASP) to understand and engineer dopant-driven phase transitions. Before that, my M.Tech thesis at NIT Hamirpur examined how surfactant chemistry controls nanoparticle size, strain, and optical defect states in Cr₂O₃.

I grew up in Rishikesh, Uttarakhand, and completed my schooling at DSB International Public School, Rishikesh, before moving on to engineering and materials research.
